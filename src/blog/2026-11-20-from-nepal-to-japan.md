After clearing my **+2** in Nepal, I decided to continue my studies abroad. In **October 2024** I arrived in Japan.

## Learning the language first

The first step was Japanese. I studied the language until I cleared it — and every day of it made life here a little easier, from reading train signs to talking with classmates.

## Studying at KCG

Now I’m studying at **Kyoto Computer Gakuin**, where I work with **Python, HTML and CSS**. Outside class I do **editing** work, which has taught me a lot about patience and finishing things properly.
